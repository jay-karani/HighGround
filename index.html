HighGround

Are you paying for flood insurance you might not need?

Government flood maps put some homes in high-risk flood zones even if the house sits above the official flood line. If that's your home, you can ask to have the map fixed for free, and your mortgage lender would no longer have to make you buy flood insurance. HighGround checks your home within seconds.

HighGround is a free flood-map screening tool for homeowners. It is not an official FEMA product or a flood zone determination.
