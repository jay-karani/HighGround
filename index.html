HighGround

Are you paying for flood insurance you might not need?

Government flood maps put some homes in high-risk flood zones even if the house sits above the official flood line. If that's your home, you can ask to have the map fixed for free, and your mortgage lender would no longer have to make you buy flood insurance. HighGround checks your home within seconds.

  1. We find your home on the flood map FEMA, the federal agency that makes flood maps, decides which homes are at-risk and how high a potential serious flood would reach.
  2. We measure the ground around your house We use LIDAR laser scans taken from airplanes by the U.S. Geological Survey to find the lowest ground touching your property.
  3. If your house is higher, you can ask FEMA to fix the map The request is called a LOMA. FEMA reviews it for free, and a land surveyor confirms the height.
